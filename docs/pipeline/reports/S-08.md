## Findings
- Verdict: **reduced** — viable only as a sideloaded APK with `targetSdkVersion 28`; such an app still gets
  reassociate/disconnect/reconnect/setWifiEnabled on Android 10–17 (AOSP source), no password, no root. Play (target ≥ 35/36) = detect-only → dropped.
- Android's own gap: Wi-Fi associated without internet → it switches to mobile data and re-validates with backoff up to 10 min;
  it does not recover the Wi-Fi itself. Clean AP outage: it rescans every 20–160 s and rejoins alone (like macOS S-07).
- `reportNetworkConnectivity` is a legal "re-validate now"; RSSI is readable without location, SSID/networkId are not.
- Doze applies to every target: frequent probing only with the screen on or charging.
- `core::probe`'s `bind_device_by_index` won't work on Android (SO_BINDTODEVICE needs CAP_NET_RAW) → `android_setsocknetwork`.
- `Cargo.toml` pulls `tray-icon`/`tao` unconditionally → must be gated for an Android build.
- Risks: OEM ROMs may differ (D1); app `disconnect()` may suppress auto-join like S-01 (D2); Google may close the legacy-target path later.
