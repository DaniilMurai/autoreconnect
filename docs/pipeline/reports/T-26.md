## Findings
- A fresh association = Associated arriving while health is NotAssociated or WifiOffByUser; the settle starts at that observation.
- Only `Refused` is newly skipped; `NotReady` was already never counted. Timeouts during the settle still count, so a
  real outage right after a join is not hidden.
- After the user's power-on the policy still asks for a Rejoin while Disassociated (when the credential is available),
  as on any drop; it races the OS's own join but is harmless.
