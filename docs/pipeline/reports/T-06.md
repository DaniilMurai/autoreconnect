## Findings
- Without `set_capabilities(true)` a drop logs `connectivity_lost` and only waits (link poll); no Rejoin, no PowerCycle (`disassociated_without_credential_waits_without_acting`).
- Disassociated inside `own_power_cycle_window` after our PowerCycle is treated as part of it (no event, no Rejoin), like PoweredOff; otherwise every power-cycle would log a false `not_associated` and race the OS auto-join.
- Rejoin does not touch `min_action_gap`/`last_action_at` (the contract limits that gap to Reassociate/PowerCycle). Back on Associated the state goes to Unknown and the next good probe logs `connectivity_restored` with the full downtime.
- Pause clears the outage start, failure count, probe window and rejoin schedule; the PowerCycle backoff survives, so pause/resume cannot be used to skip it. Repeated Pause/Resume are no-ops.
