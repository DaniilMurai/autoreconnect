- Model assumption: each probe is lost with p = 0.4 on its own; real HTTP sends ≥ 2 packets and fails more often.
- What's implemented: Degraded on ≥ 3 bad in 6 when a probe got through, and leaving it needs a clean window. Actions in a degraded episode are ≥ 90 s apart, kept through NoInternet flaps; the schedule is forgotten after 90 s Healthy. A short lost spell stays in the window as one bad probe.
